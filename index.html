About

I’m Martin, a software engineer in Trondheim. At Aker Solutions I work on industrial software and automation, which is a fancy way of saying I try to make complicated systems slightly less complicated — with mixed but occasionally promising results.

Alongside that, I study Applied Physics and Mathematics at NTNU. It sounds impressive until you see me at 2am arguing with a partial derivative that clearly started the fight.

Outside of work and school, I build things that are technically unnecessary but deeply satisfying — little games, graphics experiments, tools I could’ve just downloaded. It’s how I learn, and an excellent excuse to justify another mechanical keyboard.
